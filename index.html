Five Rolls, Two Dice: X and Y

Each student rolls a blue die (X) and a red die (Y) five times. We record the sum of X, the sum of Y, and the total. Students 1–50 are male and 51–100 are female. The histogram shows the total for every student; the scatter plot shows sum of X against sum of Y for all 100 students.

Bias scenarios
Students 1–50 are male and 51–100 are female. Every die value a female student rolls is reduced by 0.5, so her total drops by 5 (10 dice × 0.5). This is a small, systematic bias against one group.
−0.5
If a student's first roll adds up to more than 10 (an 11 or a 12), then on rolls 2–5 any die that lands on 1, 2 or 3 is rolled again, once. These trust fund kids keep an advantage for the rest of the game.
1–3
After each roll, a student's savings (running total above 10) earn 20% interest, which is added to the total and earns interest itself on later rolls.
20%
Switches and sliders apply to the next class you roll.

Gini coefficient

Left: the Lorenz curve, the share of all points held by the poorest students, from the bottom up.
